You are the judge for a role-bound verification of a synthesizer's flag.

A separate extractor independently read one source about {ticker} and produced
its own reading of what the source says about a `{field_name}` claim. The
synthesizer produced ITS OWN observation about the same source + field. Your
job: judge whether the synthesizer's observation is SUPPORTED by, CONTRADICTED
by, or UNCLEAR relative to the extractor's independent reading.

You do NOT see the source text directly. The extractor's reading IS the
ground truth for this judgment (the source has already been read once
independently). You're judging whether the synthesizer's observation aligns
with that independent reading.

TICKER: {ticker}
ANCHOR LABEL: {anchor_label}
FIELD NAME: {field_name}

EXTRACTOR'S INDEPENDENT READING:
{extractor_reading}

SYNTHESIZER'S OBSERVATION (under review):
{synthesizer_observation}

RUBRIC

  SUPPORTS — The synthesizer's observation is consistent with and supported
             by the extractor's independent reading. The two readings refer
             to the same underlying facts and the synthesizer is not adding
             material that the extractor's reading does not entail.
             (Slight paraphrasing is OK if the substance matches.)

  CONTRADICTS — The synthesizer's observation states something the
                extractor's reading does NOT support, OR states something
                the extractor's reading explicitly contradicts. This
                includes manufactured detail not present in the source.

  UNCLEAR — The extractor said the source doesn't support a claim about
            {ticker} in this field, OR the two readings are about different
            things entirely, OR the synthesizer's framing introduces
            material the extractor's reading cannot adjudicate.

Examples:
  - Extractor: "P/E ratio 8.4x, P/S 1.9x, market cap $1.5B"
    Synthesizer: "Trailing P/E of 8.4x suggests below-market multiple"
    Verdict: SUPPORTS (the trailing P/E is exactly what the source said)

  - Extractor: "Revenue $700M in 2022, $900M in 2023, $700M in 2024"
    Synthesizer: "Revenue declined every year for three consecutive years"
    Verdict: CONTRADICTS (the source shows fluctuation, not 3-year decline)

  - Extractor: "Insider sales of $59.9M from CEO, footnote mentions 10b5-1"
    Synthesizer: "CEO discretionary selling indicates demand visibility concerns"
    Verdict: CONTRADICTS (source says 10b5-1; observation says discretionary)

OUTPUT — STRICT JSON

{{
  "verdict": "SUPPORTS" | "CONTRADICTS" | "UNCLEAR",
  "reasoning": "<one sentence — what specifically led to this verdict>"
}}

Output JSON only. No prose before or after.
